# FINESST guide intro: evaluator notes

- Task ID: `finesst-guide-intro`
- Rubric: `0.3`; grader policy: `strict-v1`

## Provenance

Facts from `ktwu01.github.io` post `2024-10-20-nasa-finesst-guide-zh.md`: the award terms, the five supported divisions, and the page counts for each component. Failing fragments are **authored**.

## Target

Primary: **fidelity on the writer's own standing**. The prompt states the writer applied once and did not win, and that the advice comes from reading past successful cases and reviewer comments. A guide intro invites borrowing authority: implying experience the writer lacks, or quietly dropping the admission.

Secondary: **S5**, turning a funding program into an opportunity nobody can afford to miss.

## Required facts

- Funds graduate student research: 50,000 USD per year, up to 3 years.
- Supported areas: Earth Science, Planetary Science, Astrophysics, Heliophysics, Biological & Physical Sciences.
- Anonymized components: Science/Technical/Management 6 pages, Open Science and Data Management Plan 2 pages, Mentoring Plan 2 pages, plus references.
- Non-anonymized: Research Readiness Statement, 1 page.
- The guide breaks the process into followable steps.
- The writer applied once and did not win.
- The advice comes from past successful cases and reviewer comments the writer read.

Do not invent an acceptance rate, a deadline, a reviewer's identity, a success story, or a claim that following the guide improves odds.

## Failing fragments

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `根据我的申请经验，评审最看重的是研究问题的清晰度。` | Fidelity | Claims experience as a basis for judgment. The writer applied once without winning; the advice comes from reading others' cases. |
| `每年 5 万美元、连续 3 年，这是地球与空间科学博士生必须争取的黄金机会。` | S5 | 必须争取 and 黄金机会 assert an obligation and a superlative the terms do not carry. |
| `这份指南将帮助你在激烈的竞争中脱颖而出。` | S5; also fidelity | Promises an outcome. Nothing supplies competitiveness data or an effect. |
| `申请材料分为匿名和非匿名两类，两类材料的要求不同，需要分别准备。` | S4 | Three clauses for one fact the following list states concretely. |
| `我虽然没有中，但正因为如此，我更清楚哪里容易出问题。` | S5 | Converts a non-win into superior insight. The source claims no such thing. |

## Legitimate controls

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `我自己申请过一次，没有中。` | Fidelity pass | The supplied fact, stated plainly. Admitting it is required, and stating it once is not self-deprecation to be softened. |
| `建议来自我读过的往年成功案例和评审意见。` | Fidelity pass | Correctly sources the advice. |
| `STM 6 页，OSDMP 2 页，Mentoring Plan 2 页，另加参考文献。` | S4 pass | Four distinct component specifications. |

## Task fitness and length

An intro placed before the guide, for PhD students in the department, 600 to 1000 characters. Reproducing the whole guide is not required.
