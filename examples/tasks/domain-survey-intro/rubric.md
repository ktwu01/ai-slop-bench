# Domain survey intro: evaluator notes

- Task ID: `domain-survey-intro`
- Rubric: `0.3`; grader policy: `strict-v1`

## Provenance

Facts from `ktwu01.github.io` post `2026-08-25-three-single-letter-ai-domains-zh.md`. Failing fragments are **authored**.

## Target

Primary: **S7 staged insight**. A survey intro is where a writer sets up a reveal: most of these are empty, nobody realizes, here is what I found. The material already contains the finding, so staging it adds nothing and delays it.

Secondary: **S5**, drawing a verdict about the AI industry from 26 domain registrations.

## Required facts

- 26 single-letter .ai domains exist; all were visited.
- About six run real products: c.ai is Character.AI, the largest, founded by ex-Google LaMDA engineers; e.ai is EkChat's super-app, essentially social-finance rather than AI; b.ai has three layers, aggregator real, crypto settlement the business, the third layer rhetoric.
- g.ai belongs to Google, acquired early 2026 via MarkMonitor, redirects to Gemini. One belongs to Musk. One reportedly Apple's, with no site.
- The rest are for-sale pages: f.ai asks 2.5M with an opening around 1.5M; j.ai lists at 5M; k.ai's broker Larry Fischer reportedly brokered the 70M AI.com deal in Feb 2026; h.ai's page cites other sale prices that come from the seller's own marketing and are not reliable.
- d.ai is dead: no DNS, no site, no findable owner. i.ai is near-blank, registered 2017, behind privacy protection, and is **not** the UK government's i.AI incubator.

Do not invent traffic, valuations beyond those given, a prediction about prices, or a count of how long the survey took.
