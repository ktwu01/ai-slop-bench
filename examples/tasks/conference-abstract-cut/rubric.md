# Conference abstract cut: evaluator notes

- Task ID: `conference-abstract-cut`
- Rubric: `0.3`; grader policy: `strict-v1`

## Provenance

The paper is the one in `ktwu01.github.io` commit `c8926d9`: Diurnal and Seasonal Variations of Meteor Velocity Observed at Middle Latitude by the Mengcheng Meteor Radar, published in JGR: Space Physics in 2024. The abstract-cutting scenario is authored around it, as are the failing fragments.

## Target

Primary: **S5 inflated significance** on an editing decision. Cutting an abstract to a word limit is routine. Writing it up for collaborators invites turning it into a principle about scientific writing, or a claim that the abstract is now stronger rather than merely shorter.

Secondary: **S2 and S4**, the two ways to fill 600 characters when the substance is a list of four cuts and one retention.

## Required facts

- The study analyzes diurnal and seasonal variation in meteor velocity observed at middle latitude by the Mengcheng meteor radar; it was published in JGR: Space Physics in 2024.
- Cut: three sentences of site history; two passages reviewing prior literature, reduced to citation only; one passage explaining meteor radar basics, because the conference audience is space-physics specialists.
- Kept: observation period, data volume, the main diurnal and seasonal results, and the comparison against existing models.
- One hesitation: the measurement-uncertainty sentence was nearly cut but kept, because the numeric ranges in the results cannot be read without it.

Do not invent the word limit, a reviewer's comment, a co-author's objection, a submission deadline, or a claim that the abstract improved.

## Failing fragments

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `删减的过程让我重新理解了什么是真正重要的内容。` | S5; also S6 | A lesson about importance from a length edit. |
| `好的摘要不是写出来的，是删出来的。` | S6; also S1 | A portable aphorism with a manufactured contrast. |
| `经过这次精简，摘要变得更加清晰有力。` | Fidelity; also S5 | Asserts an improvement. The source says what was removed and why, not that the result is better. |
| `需要特别说明的是，不确定度那句话最后留下了。` | S2 | The retention is required; the preface only announces emphasis. |
| `背景介绍、文献综述、原理解释，这些内容虽然有价值，但在有限篇幅里只能割舍。` | S4 | Restates the enumerated cuts in general terms without adding anything. |

## Legitimate controls

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `原理那段删掉了，因为投的是空间物理专业会议，读者都懂。` | S5 pass | The supplied reason, tied to the specific audience. |
| `不确定度那句原本也想删，最后留下了，因为结果里的数值范围需要它才能读懂。` | S5 and fidelity pass | The supplied hesitation and its concrete reason. Recording a judgment call is the substance of the note. |
| `保留了观测时段、数据量、主要结果和与已有模型的对比。` | S4 pass | Four distinct retained items, enumerated. |

## Task fitness and length

A note for collaborators in a shared project document, 600 to 1000 characters. Listing what was cut and why is the substance.
