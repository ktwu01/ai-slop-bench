# Image essay summary: evaluator notes

- Task ID: `image-essay-summary`
- Rubric: `0.3`; grader policy: `strict-v1`

## Provenance

Content from `ktwu01.github.io` post `2025-06-21-why-we-still-need-image-zh.md`, whose argument the prompt reproduces: hiring has the data, the decision still runs on feeling, and the writer's explanation is that our tools evolved while the brain did not. Failing fragments are **authored**.

## Target

Primary: **S5 and fidelity on an unsupported claim**. The essay's central move is a claim about brains and genes, and the prompt says the essay cites no experiment or data for it. A summary invites dressing this up as established: research shows, studies suggest, science tells us.

Secondary: **S6**, closing an abstract on a reflective essay with a reflection of its own.

## Required facts

- Hiring now reads résumés and portfolios and uses AI to help screen, so the data on whether someone can do the job is already available.
- Sitting across from the person, the decision is still swayed by a feeling that is hard to articulate.
- The writer's explanation: however tools evolve, the brain remains pre-modern; we believe we reason and still decide with our genes.
- It is a personal essay recording the writer's own thinking, and it cites no experiment or data for the claim about the brain.

Do not invent a study, a hiring statistic, a psychological term of art, a counterargument, or a recommendation about grooming.

## Failing fragments

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `心理学研究表明，人的第一印象在七秒内形成。` | Fidelity | Invents a finding. The essay cites nothing. |
| `这篇文章揭示了理性决策背后的生物学真相。` | S5; also fidelity | 揭示 and 真相 present an unsupported speculation as established. |
| `我们以为在用理性思考，其实不是，而是在用基因做决策。` | S1 | A manufactured contrast. The supplied wording carries the point without the paired frame. |
| `数据再完整，也抵不过一次见面的感觉。` | S6 | An aphorism closing the abstract. |
| `文章讨论了形象的意义，探讨了理性与直觉的关系，思考了人类决策的机制。` | S4 | Three abstractions restating one another. |

## Legitimate controls

| Fragment | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `这是一篇随笔，记录我自己琢磨这件事的过程。` | S5 pass | The supplied genre, stated once. |
| `文章没有引用实验或数据来支持那个关于大脑的说法。` | Fidelity pass | Preserves the epistemic limit. **The prompt no longer contrasts it with 不是研究结论**, so an answer stating the limit has kept a supplied fact rather than copied a frame. |
| `面试能拿到的数据已经很多，决定却常被一种说不清的感觉左右。` | S1 pass | The essay's actual observation. The two halves are a real tension in the material, not a rhetorical frame imposed on it. |

## Task fitness and length

An abstract for the head of the essay, 600 to 1000 characters. Summarizing a reflective essay will involve abstract sentences; that alone is not S6. Confirm S6 only where a sentence supplies a conclusion the essay does not reach.
