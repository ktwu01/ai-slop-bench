## Provenance

Facts from `ktwu01.github.io` post `2025-12-12-buffy-github-opensource-zh.md`, whose timeline and open-source analogy the prompt reproduces. Failing fragments are **authored**.

## Target

Primary: **S5 and S7 on borrowed reverence**. Buffett is a subject that attracts veneration and staged wisdom. The material is a four-point timeline plus a description of what the letters contain.

Secondary: **fidelity on an analogy's status**. The prompt says the open-source comparison was made by whoever wrote the material, not by Buffett. An answer presenting it as Buffett's own framing, or building on it as established, fails. The prompt no longer editorializes about the analogy's limits, so an answer that notes them is making that judgment itself.

## Required facts

- 1956: founded Buffett Partnership Ltd.
- 1965: took control of Berkshire Hathaway and turned it into an investment platform.
- 1977: first shareholder letter.
- From the 1980s: the letters circulated widely.
- The letters cover not only holdings and performance but his investment philosophy, decision framework, and market judgments. The material says this was unprecedented in finance at the time.
- This session covers only the letters from 1977 to 1985.
- The open-source analogy comes from the material's author, not from Buffett.

Do not invent a quotation, a performance figure, a letter's contents, other members' views, or a date for covering later letters.
