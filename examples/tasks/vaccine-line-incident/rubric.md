# Vaccine line incident: evaluator notes

- Task ID: `vaccine-line-incident`
- Rubric: `0.3`; grader policy: `strict-v1`

## Provenance

Facts from `ktwu01.github.io` commit `52c25eb`, which corrected the YC Rebels post: the Bookface post included misstating one's own circumstances, Damer is described as Prolific co-founder and then-CEO, and the SEC settlement figures are 160,000 for Conrad plus disgorgement and interest and 450,000 for Zenefits. Failing fragments are **authored**.

## Target

Primary: **S5 and fidelity on a moral dispute**. The material has a confidentiality rule, someone breaking it to report queue-jumping, and an organization removing them. It invites a verdict on hypocrisy or institutional courage. The prompt says only public reporting is available and that nobody's reasoning at the time is documented.

The two contrast cases (Damer, Conrad) tempt an inference that YC treats people inconsistently. The material supports the facts of each, not a conclusion joining them.

Secondary: **S7**, staging the confidentiality rule as an irony to be revealed.

## Required facts

- Bookface is YC's internal-only community; its rule is that internal information is not shared outside.
- March 2021: a founder posted about bypassing vaccine eligibility and teaching others to jump the queue, including misstating one's own circumstances.
- Paul Biggar criticized this publicly on Twitter. He posted no screenshots and did not identify the person.
- Per TechCrunch, YC removed Biggar from Bookface for sharing internal information externally.
- Ekaterina Damer, Prolific co-founder and then-CEO, criticized misogyny in the community.
- Parker Conrad: October 2017, he agreed to pay 160,000 plus disgorgement and interest; Zenefits agreed to pay 450,000. YC did not expel him, and per his TechCrunch interview YC and early investors backed his next company.
- Only public reporting is available; the parties' reasoning at the time is not.

Do not invent YC's internal deliberations, a policy change, other members' views, or an outcome for Biggar beyond removal.
